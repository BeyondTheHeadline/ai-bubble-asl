The Fed can't fix this kind of inflation — English transcript (ASL Short)

Short answer: The Fed raising interest rates cannot create oil, produce food, or build houses. That means the Fed cannot directly fix those types of inflation. It can only reduce demand.

About this video

This page is the English transcript of the YouTube Short The Fed can't fix THIS kind of inflation, signed in American Sign Language (ASL) with on-screen English captions on the Beyond the Headline channel. It is hosted by Michael Moore, a CODA (Child of Deaf Adults) and an ASL interpreter. Full English transcript

English captions for The Fed can't fix THIS kind of inflation (Beyond the Headline / @BeyondTheHeadLineASL). Timestamps follow the published video.

The Fed raising interest rates cannot create oil. It can't produce food. It can't build houses.

That means the Fed cannot directly fix those types of inflation. It can only reduce demand. That's an important difference to recognize.
