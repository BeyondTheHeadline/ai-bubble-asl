Who pays the debt bill in the end? — English transcript (ASL Short)

Short answer: We can debate when the bill comes due and how we'll pay it, but pretending it isn't there is foolish. Someone will eventually have to pay: you, me, or our children.

About this video

This page is the English transcript of the YouTube Short Who pays the debt bill in the end?, signed in American Sign Language (ASL) with on-screen English captions on the Beyond the Headline channel. It is hosted by Michael Moore, a CODA (Child of Deaf Adults) and an ASL interpreter. He created Beyond the Headline to explain economics, financial markets, government debt, and the stories shaping our future in American Sign Language — not as financial advice.

This Short is cut from the longer video America is $40 Trillion in Debt. What Happens When the Bill Comes Due?.
